CONCAVE MIRROR OPTICS MASTERCLASS

1. The Mirror Equation

Relates focal length (f), object distance (do), and image distance (di):

1/f = 1/do + 1/di  •  R = 2f

• Real Image: di > 0, formed in front of mirror, inverted.
• Virtual Image: di < 0, formed behind mirror, upright.

2. Magnification (m)

m = -di / do = hi / ho

• m < 0: Inverted image (Real)
• m > 0: Upright image (Virtual)
• |m| > 1: Magnified • |m| < 1: Diminished

3. The 2 Principal Optical Rays

  • • Ray 1 (Parallel Ray): Incident parallel to optical axis → reflects through principal focus F.
  • • Ray 2 (Pole Ray): Incident directly at the pole P (vertex) → reflects symmetrically below axis at the exact same angle (θr = -θi).
